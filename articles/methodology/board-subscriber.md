# Board subscriber

This chair subscribes to the focus board of the nexus it sits in. On arrival, read it downward.

Call `now_view` with `chair` as this chair's map name. The week in that result is that nexus's `FOCUS.md`. Print This week in order, and then any Later item that is overdue or due within seven days. Stop there.

**Nothing else in that result is yours.** Do not print reconcile, sibling Forefronts, mail, or a borrowed `focus` board. Do not read a sibling's STATUS file as an API.

If Envoy is down, say so and skip the board. Nothing here waits on it.

## What it is for

Awareness, and only that. It tells this chair where its work sits in the principal's ranking before anyone starts. It is not a gate, it does not reorder what this chair was sat down to do, and a board item naming this node is not permission to begin it.

You do not write the board, propose a change to it, or reconcile it against this chair's own records. The board is the principal's.

Relation to `methodology/nexus-board`: the same system read in the opposite direction. That article is the upward half, where the nexus composes its pack and the principal ranks. This is the downward half, one chair reading the board of the nexus it sits in. A chair holding this one must never run that one, which is why they are separate articles rather than two sections of one.

Reason: the read costs a chair almost nothing and the alternative to naming it separately is folding it into the upward article, which would hand every node chair a detailed procedure for a job that is not theirs. Bundling the two is how a node ends up composing instructions for ranking the principal's week.
